Which model earns the most Pick'Em?

Strategy comparison · 13 Swiss stages, 5 Majors

Every strategy uses the same simulator and optimiser; only the win probabilities, the objective (P(≥5) or E[correct]) and the tie-break differ. Brackets are bootstrap 95% CIs over stages; differences are paired per stage. Highest mean: Bradley-Terry.

Decision

Keep BT (Bradley-Terry with a VRS prior, optimised for P(5 or more correct)) as the default. Over 13 Swiss stages from five Majors it has the highest mean correct picks (4.31 of 10) and it was highest or tied for highest on all three simulation seeds tried at 100k sims (4.31, 4.38, 4.31). The edge is small: +0.15 picks per stage over VRS seeds alone, with a paired 95% CI of -0.54 to +0.92, so the data cannot tell BT from VRS-only, the BT+VRS ensembles or the seed-anchored tie-break. No alternative beats BT either, and BT has the best Brier score (0.227 vs 0.244 for VRS), so there is no reason to switch. Maximising expected correct picks instead of P(5 or more) never helped and usually cost a coin or two.
